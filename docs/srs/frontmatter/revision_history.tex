0.2.11 & 2026-08-02 & Geovana Neves &
A stated gap replaces a partial guard. A MINOR revision adding no
requirement and withdrawing none, NARROWING REQ-41: the refusal of a
\code{concat} that discards a derivation is withdrawn, and the gap it
covered incompletely is stated in the requirement instead. The refusal
keyed on uncertainty being present at concat time, so derive, concat,
then declare reached $u = 0.36055513$ where $0.1$ is correct, with the
mechanism in place; what \code{concat} discards is the RECORD a later
declaration needs, so no concat-time test can be complete, and a guard
covering one ordering of three acts teaches that the class is handled.
The wider alternative, refusing every discarding \code{concat}
irrespective of uncertainty, would refuse ordinary data concatenation and
is left as an open product decision. The requirement now names the two
workflows that are correct. REQ-41's other three refusals and the fourth
absent-evidence case are unchanged, and
\code{UncertaintyLineageError} remains public surface. Design decision
DD-52 lands in the same window, recording the author's two JSON export
calls and the rejected \code{split\_by} alternative, all three of which
had entered as normative sentences with no decision behind them.\\[4pt]
0.2.10 & 2026-08-01 & Geovana Neves &

instead of maintained beside it. A PATCH revision adding no requirement
and withdrawing none, correcting REQ-83 and clarifying REQ-84. Design
decisions DD-48 and DD-49 land in the same window, on how
\code{itaca.\_\_version\_\_} is resolved; both modify no requirement.
REQ-84 gains the distinction between \emph{optional} in the usage
sense, a dependency the library works without, and the
\code{optional} ROLE of the table, which means declared in an extra of
\code{pyproject.toml}; of the five packages REQ-84 names, only

group is why the roles are now split: a \code{planned}
or \code{external} row is policy for something not declared yet, and it
may not name something the file does declare, so a row cannot stay in
an uncheckable state once the package becomes real. The table is

role and used-for columns, which are why it belongs in a specification
and not in a lockfile. Two floors rise in the same revision:
\code{setuptools} to 77, for PEP 639 license metadata, and
\code{pytest} to 9.0.3 with \code{pytest-cov} to 7.0, for

8.x release, so the old range held this project on a vulnerable pytest
with no remedy inside it.\\[4pt]
0.2.9 & 2026-07-31 & Geovana Neves &
The \code{.itc} becomes an authenticated canonical payload. A MINOR
revision adding no requirement and withdrawing none, widening REQ-103,
REQ-70, REQ-102, REQ-15 and REQ-48. REQ-103's scope gains the spatial coordinate system and the

the earlier rule that an unset metadata field emitted nothing is

\code{to\_json} exported without \code{allow\_draft}. REQ-70 gains the

as tamper EVIDENCE rather than tamper proofing, since the archive
carries no secret; it also gains the persisted coordinate system and
coordinate dtype, the second of which made an INTACT float32 archive
fail its own hash on reopen. The schema moves to \code{itaca-itc/3} and
its two predecessors are REFUSED by name rather than reinterpreted,
because neither records a coordinate system and reading one would
return a polar frame as cartesian, which is the defect being closed

also states what was silently untrue around the archive: REQ-70 now
requires that a CSV \code{to\_csv} writes is one \code{itc.load} reads,

REQ-48 requires that everything judgeable from an \code{.itceq} alone
is judged at parse time, since a non-finite constant and a disallowed

0.2.8 & 2026-07-31 & Geovana Neves &
The propagation engine states its scope, and refuses outside it. A MINOR
revision adding no requirement and withdrawing none, but narrowing SIX
stable ones to what the code can actually deliver: REQ-41 and REQ-36 on
the engine itself, and REQ-24, REQ-98, REQ-99 and REQ-100 on the
operations whose behavior changed with it. REQ-41 promised GUM
clause-5 propagation and said nothing about where that promise stops:
the chain rule sees ONE expression, and a derived variable is stored
with no record of the inputs that produced it, so a later operation
reading two such variables treated them as independent. Measured 3.6x
OVERSTATEMENT for \code{p = 3*x}, \code{q = 2*x}, \code{r = p - q}, and
29\% UNDERSTATEMENT for two sequential \code{translate\_moments}, on an
engine whose single-expression answers are exact. REQ-41 now states the
one-expression scope and the interim refusal
(\code{UncertaintyLineageError}), which names the equivalent single
expression rather than merely declining; the same refusal covers
\code{translate\_moments} stacked on an earlier transfer (REQ-100) and a
RANDOM-component reduction over an interpolated dimension (REQ-98,
REQ-99), and steps aside for a pair the user declared with
\code{set\_correlation}. REQ-98's preamble now says its normative table
describes what each operation does WITHIN itself and never licenses a
composition; REQ-99 states the random reduction's independence assumption,
its refusal, and why the systematic rule is untouched; REQ-100 states one
transfer per frame while uncertainty is present; REQ-24 states that
\code{concat} refuses to join a derivation record it cannot keep, since
the result carries the FIRST input's History and would assert it over
every input's rows. "Exact within one
expression" is qualified to the first order REQ-41 already works to,
everywhere it is claimed. REQ-36's non-differentiability guard extends
from a non-differentiable FUNCTION to a differentiable one evaluated at
a point where its partial does not exist: \code{abs} at zero returned
\code{u = 0} through \code{np.sign(0)}, asserting certainty where the
derivative has none. Lineage with sensitivities, which would propagate
these compositions instead of refusing them, is v0.3.0 work. Author
decision SEAT-UNC, 2026-07-31; evidence in BRF-059 (FND-058, FND-074,
FND-088, FND-095).\\

0.2.3 & 2026-07-30 & Geovana Neves &

chapter~9 states: a clarification, adding no requirement and withdrawing
none. REQ-01 gains the refusal of a CSV row WIDER than its header
(\code{DataError}, \code{R3-ITA-009}) and states the asymmetry out loud,
that a row NARROWER than its header is still NaN-filled, since a reader
who meets one half of it infers the other wrongly. The asymmetry is
deliberate: a row with more fields than the header is a malformed file,

its own error message cited REQ-01, while REQ-01 did not describe it; the

independent review REV-004 found (\code{ITC-20260729-2255}).

REQ-95 gains the specification build. Two defects had reached \code{main}
in a document that no mechanism compiled: chapter~7 carried a blank line
after every content line, and one unescaped underscore inside \code{code}

Two enumerations were false in normative text and are corrected
differently, because only one of them should exist at all. REQ-92
claimed \code{0.2.0} ships three breaking changes and it ships more; its
enumeration is REMOVED rather than recounted, and no replacement count
is put in its place, since \code{CHANGELOG.md} is where the same

three different counts across four places while

propagate; the list is now given ONCE, at five, adding \code{fill} with
\code{method="polyfit"}, and REQ-26 gains the matching per-method split.
The ground recorded for \code{fill(method="polyfit")} is that the fill
path emits no weights for the propagation to consume. Explicitly not
that its weights are data dependent, which was measured false and would
have drawn \code{interpolate} into the family with it; and explicitly
not that no polynomial weight rule exists, since one ships and
\code{interpolate} propagates through it. Whether that matrix is
admissible over a support chosen per gap is OQ-42, opened here, and it
may return the count to four.

This is a document-version BUMP rather than a same-baseline refinement,
for the reason the 0.2.1 entry established and one more. Nine \stable{}
requirements change text, the six listed above for the refusals and
REQ-92, REQ-98 and REQ-26 for the two enumerations, which is well past
the threshold that entry set; and a reader holding v0.2.0 needs to be
able to name which document version first described the refusals that
release actually shipped. The argument does not rest on the count being
exact, which is why it is given with its list beside it.\\
